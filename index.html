Section 1Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Unfollow All on TikTok (“the Extension”) uses and stores information when you use it to view the accounts you follow on TikTok and unfollow the accounts you choose. The Extension works entirely inside your browser. Your TikTok account data is never sent to the developer, sold or shared.

Section 2Information the Extension Accesses

  • Your Following and Friends lists: read on tiktok.com so the Extension can show them in its own list, split into Following, Friends and Not following back, and unfollow only the accounts you choose.
  • Your followers list: read only when you run a follower check, so it can be compared with your last check to show who unfollowed you.
  • Account details: when you open an account’s details, the Extension shows that account’s public profile information, such as followers, following, videos and verification.
  • Your signed-in session: the Extension uses your existing TikTok login to carry out the actions you request. It never asks for, reads or stores your password.
  • License key: if you buy the Pro version, the key you enter is used only to confirm your purchase.

All TikTok requests go directly to TikTok, in the same way the TikTok website works. The Extension works only on tiktok.com.

Section 3How the Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the Extension’s features:

  • Showing your Following list with search, filters, sorting and selection presets.
  • Unfollowing the lists or accounts you choose, while always skipping locked accounts.
  • Showing live progress and pausing automatically when TikTok asks for a short break.
  • Showing who unfollowed you after each follower check.
  • Creating the CSV backup of your Following list and the exports of accounts you unfollowed (CSV, JSON, TXT and HTML).

Everything is processed on your own device, in your browser. No account data is sent to the developer or to any analytics service.

Section 4Data Storage and Retention

The following information is saved only in your browser, on your device:

  • Your settings, including theme.
  • Locked (kept) accounts.
  • The current run’s progress and list counts.
  • Follower-check snapshots and the list of who unfollowed you.
  • Your license key and activation status.

A free-use counter is also saved in Chrome’s sync storage, which Chrome may copy to your other browsers signed in to the same Google account. Backups and exports are saved only as files on your own device.

Data in the Extension’s storage is kept only while the Extension is installed and is deleted when you uninstall it. Files you have saved stay on your device until you delete them.

Section 5Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone, with one exception: checking your license. When you activate the Pro version, and each time you open the Extension’s popup after that, your license key and the product ID are sent to Gumroad, our payment provider, only to confirm that your license is valid. No TikTok data or any other information is included.

Section 6What We Never Do

  • Sell, rent or transfer user data to third parties.
  • Use data for advertising, profiling or to determine creditworthiness.
  • Use or transfer data for any purpose unrelated to managing the accounts you follow on TikTok.
  • Collect analytics, track your browsing or load code from outside the Extension.

Section 7Children’s Privacy

The Extension is not intended for children.
